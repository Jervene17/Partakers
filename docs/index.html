📖 Guide to the bot

Three things to know

Tap, don't type

Almost everything is done with the buttons under the bot's messages.

Stuck? Start over

Send /cancel to stop what you're doing, then /menu to begin again. Lost? Tap 📖 Guide to the bot in the menu to open this page.

The bot is fair

It counts everyone's roles and gives the next one to whoever has the fewest. It avoids giving anyone two roles on the same day.

For everyone

If you take part in services, this is the only part you need.

1. Tell the bot the dates you can't do

Before each month's schedule is made, the admin opens a "preference round". The bot posts a message in the group with a link called Set my preferences.

  1. Tap the link. It opens a private chat with the bot.

  2. Tap your name.

  3. Tap every date you can NOT do. It shows a ✖. Tap it again to undo.

  4. Not available for the whole month? Tap ✖ I'm not available this month instead of tapping every date one by one.

  5. Tap I'm done. Your dates are saved.

Good to know

Dates you don't tap count as available. A 🎤 next to a date means you are the preacher that day, so you are already free from other roles. You can open the same link again to change your dates any time before the deadline. If you miss the deadline, you are treated as available on every date.

2. After the schedule is out

Plans change. Open /menu and tap 🔄 Adjustments ›. Always ask the other person first, then use the bot to make it official. The group is told about every change.

I can't do my roleCancel a role and pick someone to cover.

Tap Cancel a role (or send /cancel_role).

  1. Pick the service, then tap your name.

  2. Tap the role and date you want to give up.

  3. The bot lists people who are free and able to do it. Ask one of them first.

  4. Tap that person's name, then tap Confirm.

Changed your mind? Tap Never mind, keep my role. Nothing changes until you confirm.

If the bot says nobody is available, tell the Service Director right away.

Two people want to trade datesSwap dates: the same role on two different dates.

Tap Swap dates (or send /swap).

  1. Pick the service and the role.

  2. Tap the first date, then the date to swap with.

  3. The bot shows who will be on each date after the swap. Tap Confirm swap.

If someone would end up with two roles on the same day, the bot warns you first. You can tap Swap + move their other role and the bot gives their other role to a free person, fairly. Or tap Swap anyway (keep both roles), or Cancel.

Someone will cover me for one dateSubstitute a partaker for one role on one date.

Tap Substitute a partaker (or send /substitute).

  1. Pick the service, the role and the date.

  2. Tap the person who will cover. The list only shows people who are free that day.

  3. If that person already has another role that day, the bot asks if you still want to proceed.

On days the service is relayed from another church, you can pick 📡 Live broadcast instead of a person.

I'm too busy this monthSpecial request: limit how many roles you get.

Tap Special request (or send /special_request).

  1. Pick the service, then your name, then the month.

  2. Choose the most roles you can do: 1, 2 or 3.

The bot keeps your earliest roles and gives the extra ones to other people, fairly.

3. Look up a schedule

See a service's scheduleNearest date, this week (Predawn) or this month.

Tap 🔎 Pull schedule, pick the service, then pick the period. Tap 📅 Pick a month to see any month, past or future, not just the nearest date or the current month.

See my own scheduleEverything you are assigned to.

Tap 👤 Pull person and tap your name. Then choose Schedule Based on Service (all your dates in one service) or Schedule for the Month (everything in one month, across all services).

For the admin

Whoever runs the schedule (the Service Director). Do the steps below in this order each month.

Your monthly routine

  1. Set the preachers. Tap 🎤 Set preacher for Sunday and Wednesday. For Predawn, use 🌄 Set Predawn pattern.

  2. Do Filipino Translation first. Open a preference round for it, close it, then generate its schedule. Sunday has to wait for this.

  3. Open a preference round for Sunday and for Wednesday. Partakers mark the dates they can't do.

  4. Close each round when everyone has replied. If you forget, it closes itself at the deadline.

  5. Generate the schedule for Sunday and Wednesday. Then generate Sun Stop Sundays, which needs no round.

  6. Log the Tech people. Tech is never picked by the bot. You choose.

  7. Share the result. Copy or forward the schedule to the group if it isn't already there.

If the bot says "Can't generate yet", it isn't broken. It tells you exactly which step is missing, usually the preacher or the preference round.

Do once when you start

Register the groupsSo the bot knows where to post announcements and reminders.

Open each Telegram group, send /set_group_chat there, and pick what it's for. Do this once per group. Preference announcements and reminders won't work until it's done.

  • Service Partakers: a general group for everyone, used for most announcements.
  • Filipino Translators: the Filipino Translation team's own group.
  • A role, such as Presider, Representative Prayer or Praise Leader: for a group dedicated to just that role, like "LGC Presiders". When you open a preference round for a service, every group registered for one of that service's roles gets the announcement too, alongside Service Partakers. Wednesday has no Praise Leader role right now, so a Praise Leader group is only messaged for Sunday.

A group can only hold one purpose. Register as many role groups as you like.

👥 RosterAdd or remove people, and choose which roles each person can do.

Tap 👥 Roster. You can:

  • Add new member: type their name.
  • Add member to a role: pick service, role, then the person.
  • Remove member from a role: pick service, role, then the person.
  • Remove member from all roles: for someone who has left or is away.

Names must be spelled the same way everywhere.

➕ Add service and ➕ Add roleCreate a new kind of service, or add one more role to an existing one.

Add service: type the name, say whether it happens monthly or weekly, add each role, and list who can do each one. For every role you choose:

  • Randomize: the bot picks people fairly.
  • Manual entry only: you choose the person yourself, like Preacher and Tech.

At the end the bot offers to generate a schedule now, or to give you a template to fill in.

Add role: pick the service, type the role name and who can do it, then choose Randomize or Manual.

Each month

🎤 Set preacherChoose the preacher for each Sunday or Wednesday.

  1. Tap 🎤 Set preacher and pick Sunday or Wednesday.

  2. Pick the month.

  3. For each date, tap the preacher. The usual one is marked (default).

The preacher is kept free of other roles that day. If you pick someone who already has a role that day, the bot asks you to confirm. On days the service comes live from another church, pick 📡 Live broadcast.

🌄 Set Predawn patternSet the weekly preachers once, then reuse them every month.

Predawn runs Monday to Saturday. Tap 🌄 Set Predawn pattern and pick the preacher for each weekday. The bot then offers to create a month from it. Next time, just choose Generate schedule then Predawn. You only redo the pattern when the arrangement changes.

🗳 Preferences: open and close a roundCollect the dates people can't do.

Open a round:

  1. Tap 🗳 Preferences › then Open a preference round.

  2. Pick the service and the month.

  3. Pick a deadline: 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks (each ending at 8:00 PM), or type your own date and time as 2026-10-15 20:00.

The bot posts the announcement and link in the Service Partakers group.

Close early: tap Close a round early and pick the round. The bot tells the group that it's closed. Generating the schedule only works after the round is closed.

Sunday's round can't open until Filipino Translation's round for that month is closed, and the preachers must be set first.

📅 Generate scheduleFills in a whole month, fairly.

  1. Tap 📅 Generate schedule.

  2. Pick the service and the month.

  3. The bot shows the finished schedule.

It avoids dates people marked as unavailable. Anything already filled in is kept, so running it twice never creates doubles. If someone had to be placed on a date they marked, the bot warns you.

Pick Filipino Translation before Sunday, so the Filipino Preacher isn't also given Presider that day.

The bot also tries to keep the Filipino Preacher mainly just preaching — it avoids also giving them Initial Proofreading on another Sunday that month, though 2nd PR sometimes is fine.

The Filipino Preacher translates the sermon online while the Preacher speaks in English, so they are always two different people. The bot will never make the Sunday Preacher the Filipino Preacher, whether it is generating, setting a preacher, or swapping. If you type it in by hand in the Dashboard or a bulk upload, the bot warns you.

🎛 Log tech and 📝 Log a roleFor roles that you choose by hand.

Log tech is for Onsite Tech (and Online Tech at Predawn). Pick the service, the role, and the month (or the week, for Predawn). Then tap a person for each date.

Log a role works the same way for any hand-picked role in a service you created. It also offers 📡 Live broadcast.

If someone already has another role that day, the bot asks you to confirm.

Changes and lookups

🔄 AdjustmentsCancel, swap, substitute, special request, live broadcast.

The first four work exactly as described in the "For everyone" part above. As the admin, you also have one more:

Mark live broadcast: for a day the service is relayed from another church. Pick the service, month and date. The bot sets every role that day to Live broadcast (Tech is left alone) and tells the group. It replaces anything already assigned that day, and it isn't available for Sun Stop Sundays.

🔎 Pull schedule and 👤 Pull personLook things up.

Pull schedule: a service's nearest date, this week (Predawn only), or this month.

Pull person: everything one person is assigned to, by service or by month.

Spreadsheet tools

The bot keeps its records in a Google Sheet. You can open it any time. Please don't rename the tabs or the column headings.

📤 Bulk upload (template)Fill in a schedule offline, then send it back.

  1. Tap 📤 Bulk upload (template), pick the service and the month or week.

  2. The bot sends you a file. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets and type names in the Partaker column. Use the same spelling as the roster.

  3. Save it as a CSV and send it back to the bot in the chat.

The bot updates the schedule and tells you how many entries it changed. Empty rows are ignored.

⚙️ More: Refresh and Sync dashboardsEasy-to-read tables inside the Google Sheet.

Refresh dashboards builds a "Dashboard" tab for each service, showing the next three months with dates down the side and roles across the top.

You can type names straight into those tables. After that, tap Sync dashboards so the bot saves what you typed. If you forget, the next refresh will erase your typing. If your typing gives someone two roles on the same day, the bot tells you, so you can fix it with Swap or Substitute.

Once a year

🗓 Yearly renewalStart a fresh year.

Tap 🗓 Yearly renewal. The bot asks you to confirm, then:

  • moves this year's schedule into an Archive tab in the Google Sheet (nothing is lost),
  • clears the live schedule tabs,
  • resets everyone's fairness count to zero,
  • and refreshes the dashboards.

Do it when the year is really finished.

If something looks wrong

Most problems are fixed with the first two lines.

Buttons do nothing, or the bot seems stuckStart fresh.

Send /cancel, then /menu, and use the new buttons. Buttons on old messages can stop working.

"Google Sheets is rate-limiting me"Too many requests at once.

Wait one minute, then try once. Tapping repeatedly makes it worse.

"Something went wrong" with a code in bracketsFor example (KeyError).

Try again in a moment. If it keeps happening, send the admin a screenshot of the message, so they can find the cause.

"Can't generate yet" or "Can't open preferences yet"Not an error. A step is missing.

Read the message. It names the step to do first, such as setting the preacher or closing the Filipino Translation round.

The wrong person is scheduledFix it without starting over.

Use Substitute a partaker for one date, or Swap dates for two. You can also type the correct name in the Dashboard tab in the Google Sheet, then tap Sync dashboards.

The bot doesn't answer at allIt may be switched off.

Tell the person who looks after the bot's server. It may need to be restarted.
